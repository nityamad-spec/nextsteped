name: Teacher Setup Flow
description: 9-step professor setup pipeline order and navigation
type: feature
---
The professor setup is a 9-step pipeline: 1) Profile & Course Setup, 2) Syllabus Review, 3) Concept Management (auto-generated from approved syllabus), 4) Course Materials (upload lesson plans, past exams, homework, exercises), 5) AI Lesson Plan (generated from uploaded materials), 6) Diagnostic Questions, 7) AI Assistant (TA Settings), 8) Exam Mode, and 9) Enrollment. Concepts are extracted from the approved syllabus JSON, not from uploaded course materials. The Course Materials upload step is separate from the AI lesson plan generation step. No "workshop" terminology — use "lesson plan" and "course" instead.
