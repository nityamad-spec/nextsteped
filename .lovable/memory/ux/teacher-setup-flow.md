name: Teacher Setup Flow
description: 8-step professor setup pipeline order and navigation
type: feature
---
The professor setup is an 8-step pipeline: 1) Profile & Course Setup, 2) Syllabus Review, 3) Concept Management (auto-generated from approved syllabus), 4) Lesson Plan (upload materials + AI-generated lesson plan combined), 5) Diagnostic Questions, 6) AI Assistant (TA Settings), 7) Exam Mode, and 8) Enrollment. Concepts are extracted from the approved syllabus JSON. The Lesson Plan step combines uploading course materials (lesson plans, past exams, homework, exercises) with AI lesson plan generation. No "workshop" terminology — use "lesson plan" and "course" instead.
